Understanding Negative Numbers

What Are Negative Numbers?

Negative numbers are numbers less than zero. They are written with a minus sign (–) in front, such as –1, –5, or –100.

Example: If the temperature is 3°C and it drops by 5 degrees, it becomes –2°C.

How to Work with Negative Numbers

  • Adding a negative number is the same as subtracting: 5 + (–3) = 2
  • Subtracting a negative number is like adding: 5 – (–3) = 8
  • Multiplying two negatives makes a positive: (–4) × (–2) = 8
