Parts of Speech

1. Nouns

Names of people, places, things, or ideas. Examples: dog, city, book, happiness

2. Pronouns

Words used in place of nouns. Examples: he, she, it, they

3. Verbs

Words that show action or state of being. Examples: run, eat, is, jump

4. Adjectives

Words that describe nouns or pronouns. Examples: red, tall, happy, soft

5. Adverbs

Words that describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Examples: quickly, very, silently

6. Prepositions

Words that show relationships between nouns or pronouns and other words. Examples: in, on, under, with

7. Conjunctions

Words that join words or groups of words. Examples: and, but, or, because

8. Interjections

Words or phrases that express strong emotion. Examples: Wow!, Ouch!, Oh no!

1. "She runs very fast." — What is "runs"?

2. "Wow! That was amazing." — What is "Wow"?

3. "The book on the table is mine." — What is "on"?
