The Water Cycle

The water cycle, also known as the hydrologic cycle, describes how water moves through the Earth's atmosphere, surface, and underground. It plays a vital role in weather patterns and supports all life forms.

1. Evaporation

Water from oceans, rivers, and lakes heats up from the sun and turns into water vapor, rising into the atmosphere.

2. Condensation

As water vapor rises and cools, it changes back into liquid droplets, forming clouds.

3. Precipitation

When clouds get heavy with water droplets, they release them as rain, snow, sleet, or hail back to the Earth's surface.

4. Collection

Precipitated water collects in bodies of water like rivers, lakes, and oceans. Some also soaks into the ground and becomes groundwater.
